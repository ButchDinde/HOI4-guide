Navy guide

Have you ever had the unfortune of playing a nation in HOI4, where you have to make a navy?
Are you sick of doing sealion with paratroopers? Then this is the guide for you.
This guide focuses on Man the Guns, but I will go over some basic naval strategy for the base game.

Naval composition

The navy is as the army divided up into subgroups. An admiral can have multiple "taskforces" assigned to them. Each taskforce can be set to different tasks, and should ideally be composed for a few spesific tasks. A combat taskforce should for example have a composition of 3 screens per capital ship. For example would a taskforce of 3 destroyers and 1 heavy cruiser be ideal efficiency to ic. 4 destroyers would provide redundancy and more protection for the cruiser, but will be less efficient per ic.

Screens

You want your screens to have a decent combination of speed, light attack, and affordability. You get this through the modules of your ships in the naval designer. The most important thing however, is that you have enough of them to cover your main damage dealers, the heavy ships. The "easy" meta has for a while consisted of destroyers and heavy cruisers. Destroyers are pretty inexpensive, and can be fitted to deal a good amount of both light and torpedo damage.
There is also the option of light cruisers. They are more expensive and less cost efficient, but do deal more per ship damage. They can also soak up more damage. A task force with 3 destroyers will porbably be worse than one with 3 light cruisers. The trade off is that you can make a lot more Destroyers with the same industrial capacity.
As a smaller nation you should probably go for a bare-bones destroyer design to fill the line, as a medium sized one you can afford good destroyers, or as one of the big four naval nations (UK, USA, Japan, Italy) you could have a pretty good mix. If you are in doubt however, always do whatever is funniest.

Capital ships

Going off the same philosophy as with screens, heavy cruisers are your best bet for efficiency. They deal good amounts of damage, and have a good speed. It has a small trade off in it's survivability, but that isn't as relevant as long as you have a full screen line to block the damage. A step up is battlecruisers and battleships. They are heavier, slower, tankier, adn of course deal more damage. They are as with the destroyer/light cruisers better stat-wise, but it isn't worth the cost. At top of the list we have the super-heavy battleships. They are never worth it, but they are the funniest ships to build.

Aircraft carriers

They are kinda boring imo, but they still sit as a important part of naval meta. They have their own backline, and don't participate directly much. However, naval bombers are unfortunatley the naval meta. A navy without planes or anti-air, will always lose to one with planes. The best strategy for a sea-lion has always been bombing out the royal navy. Here we also encounter the biggest problem with the navy. You can fully circumvent it with air power, which is less expensive. They are also produced in regular military factories, so it allows for a industrial reorganization later on. You can of course not get around supply and trading issues from blockaded naval lanes, but you can always just ignore those issues.
When designing an aircraft carrier, there are a few considerations. The main one is deck space, and how many planes you carry. You also want to consider speed, as your task force moves at the speed of your slowest ships. Survivability and anti-air is also important, though the latter can be ignored for more planes, whom act as an efficient source of anti air.
You don't need aircraft carriers in a singleplayer game, as you will always win against AI with a destroyer/heavy cruiser build. For MP you do probably need them in order to beat a player. For smaller nations, they are not a priority.

Submarines

You don't need submarines. They used to provide great soft power (strike force), but after the naval rework they are mostly useful for trade interdiction. Navies without any sonars, depth charges, or their own subs, will however struggle against good submarines. Sub 4's are pretty good, and they are almost alwayes banned from "serious" MP games for this reason.
